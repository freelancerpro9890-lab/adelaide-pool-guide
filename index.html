A simple routine for Adelaide pool owners

Backyard pools can require different care depending on their size, equipment and treatment system. Still, a few habits are useful for most homeowners.

1. Look before you clean

Before starting routine cleaning, take a quick look at the water and the equipment area. Notice changes in water clarity, debris, leaks or anything that appears different from the previous visit.

2. Know what normal equipment sounds like

Pool pumps and filtration equipment make some noise during operation. What matters is a noticeable change. Grinding, rattling, repeated cycling or unusual vibration can be a reason to investigate further.

3. Keep a basic maintenance rhythm

  • Remove leaves and visible debris.
  • Check baskets and filters according to the equipment instructions.
  • Monitor water condition and follow your normal water-care routine.
  • Look for leaks around pumps, filters and plumbing.
  • Keep the pool surrounds clean and unobstructed.

4. Know when to call for help

Persistent leaks, electrical faults, equipment failures and problems that you cannot safely identify are better treated as professional-service issues. The goal isn't to fix everything yourself; it is to recognise when something needs attention.
